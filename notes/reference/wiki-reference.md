## 2. DRUGS

### 2.1 Marijuana (Weed)
- **First available drug**, immediately at game start
- **4 Strains:** OG Kush ($38), Sour Diesel ($40), Green Crack ($43), Granddaddy Purple ($44)
- **Cultivation:** Seeds → Grow Tent → Harvest (8-12 units)
- **Base Effects:** Calming, Refreshing, Energizing, Sedating

### 2.2 Methamphetamine (Meth)
- **Unlocked:** Hoodlum I
- **Supplier:** Shirley Watts (Pseudoephedrine)
- **Base Price:** $70 per crystal
- **Production:** Chemistry Station + Lab Oven (14 hours)
- **Ingredients:** Acid ($40), Phosphorus ($40), Pseudo ($60-$110)
- **Yield:** 10 crystals per batch

### 2.3 Shrooms (Mushrooms)
- **Unlocked:** Downtown Region
- **Supplier:** Fungal Phil
- **Base Price:** $65 per unit
- **Production:** Spore Syringe → Grain Bag → Mushroom Bed (12h)
- **Special:** Requires AC Units for cooling system
- **Yield:** 16-20 per bed

### 2.4 Cocaine
- **Unlocked:** Enforcer I (Docks)
- **Supplier:** Salvador Moreno
- **Base Price:** $150 per unit
- **Production:** Cultivation → Drying → Kettle → Lab Oven (20-28h + 12h)
- **Ingredients:** 20 Coca Leaves + Benzyl Acid
- **Yield:** 10 per batch ($1,255 profit)

## 4. QUALITY SYSTEM

### 4.1 Quality Levels
1. Trash (0.00-0.25)
2. Poor (0.26-0.40)
3. Standard (0.41-0.75)
4. Premium (0.76-0.90)
5. Heavenly (0.91-1.00)

### 4.2 Influencing Factors
- **PGR + Speed Grow:** -0.15 quality
- **Fertilizer:** +0.3 quality
- **Drying Rack:** +1 level per 12 game hours (max. 12 min real)
- **Heavenly:** Only achievable through drying

### 4.3 Quality Bonus
+15% per exceeded level (max. +60%)

## 9. GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

### 9.1 Controls

- **Basic:** WASD, Sprint (Shift), Crouch (Ctrl), Interact (E), Flashlight (F)
- **Vehicles:** WASD, Lights (H), Exit (E), Handbrake (Space)
- **Skateboard:** Mount/Dismount (Left Click), Ollie (hold Space)
- **Combat:** Light Attack (Left Click), Heavy (Hold), Aim (Right Click), Reload (R)
- **Inventory:** Right Click = 1 item, Shift+Left Click = entire stack

### 9.2 Phone System (Tab)

- Messages: Accept deals
- Journal: Active missions
- Products: Management & pricing
- Deliveries: Order from businesses
- Map: Show positions
- Dealers: Manage employees

### 9.3 Daily Schedule

| Time | Event |
|------|-------|
| 7:00 | Day begins |
| 16:00 | Casino opens |
| 18:00 | Shops close, Warehouse opens |
| 20:30 | Curfew warning |
| 21:00 | Curfew begins |
| 4:00 | Time pauses, employees stop |

### 9.4 Dead Drops

23 hidden locker locations in Hyland Point. Marked with "X". Suppliers deposit orders there.

### 9.5 Pickpocketing

1. Sneak behind NPC
2. Crouch
3. Press "E"
4. Stop triangle on green zone (Space)

**Tip:** Good for mixing ingredients in early game

### 9.6 Gambling (Casino, 4PM-5AM)

- **Blackjack:** Closer to 21 than dealer (Blackjack = 2.5x)
- **Slots:** EV 1.36x (player-friendly!)
  - 3 Fruits = x2, 3 same = x10, 3 Bells = x25, 3 Sevens = x100
- **Ride the Bus:** Up to x20 with optimal strategy

### 9.7 Money Economy

- **ATM Deposit:** Max $10,000/week
- **Money Laundering:** Max $20,000/24h through businesses
- **Cash Only:** Suppliers, Oscar's Store, Casino, Pawn Shop, Weapons, Employees
